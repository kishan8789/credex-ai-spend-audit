# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phase 1: Launch (Month 1)

### Positioning
**"Know Your AI Bill. Fix It in 5 Minutes."**

Target: Early-adopter founders spending $100+ on AI tools who actively manage costs.

### Launch Channels

1. **Product Hunt** (Primary)
   - Post on launch day (Thursday)
   - Prepare 3-4 media assets (dashboard screenshots, results demo)
   - Engage in comments for first 8 hours
   - Goal: Top 5 product of the day = 2,000+ visits

2. **Hacker News** (Secondary)
   - Post link in "Show HN" thread
   - Write honest, no-hype description
   - Goal: 100+ clicks, 30-40% to landing page

3. **Indie Hackers**
   - Monthly "Building in Public" post
   - Share startup story and revenue metrics
   - Goal: 50-100 signups

4. **Twitter/X** (Founder Networks)
   - Target: @paulg, @ycombinator followers
   - Highlight concrete savings numbers
   - Goal: Viral thread with 5K+ likes

### Content Calendar (Week 1)
- Monday: Product Hunt launch post
- Tuesday: Hacker News submission
- Wednesday: Indie Hackers post
- Thursday: Twitter thread with case studies
- Friday: Email to early users for testimonials

## Phase 2: Product Validation (Month 1-2)

### User Acquisition Tactics

1. **Cold Outreach** (Founders only)
   - Scrape Product Hunt makers, AngelList founders
   - Email: "5-min tool that found us $X/month savings"
   - Goal: 3% response rate = 30 signups from 1,000 outreach

2. **Reddit** (relevant communities)
   - r/startups: "I built a tool to audit AI spending"
   - r/entrepreneurs: Share results, not sales pitch
   - Goal: 100-200 organic visits

3. **LinkedIn** (Founder circles)
   - Post audit results snapshots
   - Share "founder's dilemma" content
   - Goal: 50-100 referral signups

### Feedback Loops

- **Weekly**: Contact 10 users who completed audits
  - Question: "Would you recommend this to other founders?"
  - Capture testimonial-worthy quotes

- **Bi-weekly**: Product survey (in-app)
  - What's missing?
  - Would you pay for X feature?
  - NPS measurement

### Conversion Optimization

- Landing page A/B tests
  - Variant A: Hero with savings example
  - Variant B: Hero with customer testimonial
  - Metric: Signup rate
  - Target: 15%+ signup rate

- Form optimization
  - Reduce required fields
  - Add progress indicator
  - Target: 70%+ completion rate

## Phase 3: Monetization (Month 2-3)

### Premium Tier Launch

**Freemium Model:**
- Free: Basic audit (current)
- Pro ($29/month):
  - Monthly re-audits
  - PDF reports
  - Email summaries
  - Priority recommendations updates

**Target Pricing:**
- 5-10% of active users → 50-100 paying customers
- 10 customers × $29 = $290 MRR (achievable in month 3)

### Revenue Streams

1. **Subscription** (Primary): $29/month for premium features
2. **Affiliate** (Secondary): Partner with cheaper tool providers
   - Get commission if user switches (future)
3. **API Access** (Tertiary): White-label for VC/founders

---

## Phase 4: Growth (Month 3+)

### Growth Loops

1. **Referral Loop**
   - User gets 30% discount if they share with teammate
   - Goal: 20% of users refer 1-2 others

2. **Benchmark Loop**
   - "See how you compare to similar teams"
   - Requires user to share company size, funding stage
   - Creates FOMO ("other teams save 45%")

3. **Content Loop**
   - Publish "AI cost trends" monthly report
   - Link to audit tool
   - Goal: SEO traffic, thought leadership

### Expansion into Adjacent Markets

- **SaaS managers**: "Audit all your SaaS tools"
- **AI agencies**: "Audit client AI budgets"
- **VCs**: "Portfolio company AI spending benchmarks"

## Brand Guidelines

### Tone
- **Honest**: "You're probably overspending. Here's why."
- **Actionable**: Every statement includes how-to
- **Focused**: About AI spend optimization, not general productivity

### Visual Identity
- Dark mode (technical credibility)
- Teal/cyan accent color (modern, trust)
- Clean typography (clarity over decoration)
- No cartoon illustrations (serious problem, serious solution)

### Messaging Hierarchy
1. **Headline**: The problem ("Know your AI bill")
2. **Subheading**: The value ("Fix it in 5 minutes")
3. **CTA**: The ask ("Audit for free")
