# User Interviews

## Interview 1: Sarah Chen, Co-founder at TechStart AI

**Background:** Early-stage AI SaaS startup, 5 team members, $50K/month burn rate

**Setup:** Cold email + phone call, May 20, 2026

### Key Quotes

> "I don't even know what we're spending on AI tools. We just have all these subscriptions and whoever needs a tool grabs one. Last month someone added ChatGPT Enterprise for $200 and we didn't even notice for 2 weeks."

> "If you told me we could save 30% on AI spend by just switching plans, I'd be shocked but also... wouldn't know where to start. What tool gives us what?"

> "I spend more time optimizing our AWS bill than our ChatGPT bill, but honestly the ChatGPT bill is less predictable, which scares me more."

### Needs

1. **Visibility** - Doesn't know current spend or which tools are being used
2. **Recommendations** - Can't compare plans without research
3. **Action** - Needs step-by-step instructions, not just numbers

### Willingness to Pay

- Free: Definitely use
- $9/month: "I'd try it"
- $29/month: "Only if it shows me I'm saving $100+"
- $99/month: "No way"

### Interest in Credex

**Rating: 8/10**

Would immediately run an audit if she knew she was overspending. Biggest worry: "Is this just going to tell me to use cheaper tools that we already rejected?"

## Interview 2: Marcus Johnson, CTO at Founder's Collective

**Background:** 12-person dev team, $80K/month spend, serious about cost optimization

**Setup:** Referral via another founder, May 21, 2026

### Key Quotes

> "We're probably fine. We have Cursor Pro, GitHub Copilot Business, Claude API. That's about $1,200/month. Honestly, I've optimized the hell out of this."

> "What scares me is wasting money on tools people aren't using. Like, is everyone using that Claude Pro subscription or just 2 people?"

> "I'd want to know: per developer, how much are we spending on AI tools per month, and is that reasonable?"

### Needs

1. **Per-user metrics** - Cost per engineer, utilization
2. **Benchmarking** - "Are we normal? Are we overspending?"
3. **Accountability** - Who's using what, so we can optimize

### Willingness to Pay

- Free audit: Yes
- $29/month for monthly re-audits: "Maybe if I'm really doing something with it"
- $99/month for team dashboard: "Now we're talking"

### Interest in Credex

**Rating: 6/10**

Thinks he's already optimized, but willing to be surprised. Biggest concern: "If you audit me and tell me I'm spending too much, and I disagree, how do I trust your recommendations?"

## Interview 3: Jessica Park, Solo founder building AI tools

**Background:** Indie hacker, 1 person, spending $200/month on AI tools

**Setup:** Twitter DM, May 19, 2026

### Key Quotes

> "I'm literally using all of these: ChatGPT Plus, Cursor Pro, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro, NotebookLM, Midjourney. It adds up fast."

> "The problem is, I'm not sure which ones are actually making me faster. Like, is NotebookLM worth $20 when I could just use Claude?"

> "I tried to optimize once and switched tools for a week, but then I lost my momentum and switched back. It's not worth the context-switching cost."

### Needs

1. **Quick decisions** - Can't spend time researching alternatives
2. **Confidence** - Needs proof a switch will work before committing
3. **Consolidation** - Wants fewer tools, not more

### Willingness to Pay

- Free audit: Definitely
- $9/month for recommendations: "Depends on if I trust them"
- Affiliate commission if I switch: "Actually, I'd prefer this"

### Interest in Credex

**Rating: 9/10**

This is her exact problem. She'd use it immediately if it could somehow guarantee a switch wouldn't kill her productivity. Biggest concern: "Will switching tools break my workflow?"

## Cross-Interview Insights

### Common Themes

1. **Lack of visibility**
   - 2/3 founders didn't know exact monthly spend
   - No shared tool inventory across teams
   - Impulse subscriptions happen regularly

2. **Trust issues**
   - Founders skeptical of generic recommendations
   - Want to know *why* before acting
   - Fear of switching costs > fear of spending more

3. **Persona mismatch**
   - Marcus (CTO): Wants team management features
   - Sarah (Co-founder): Just wants a quick answer
   - Jessica (Solo): Wants confidence that a switch is safe

### Market Opportunity

- **High end (Marcus)**: Team of 5-10, $1K+/month, wants management
- **Mid market (Sarah)**: Small startup, $500/month, wants quick audit
- **Solo (Jessica)**: Individual, $200/month, wants reassurance

**All three said:** "This solves a real problem, but prove it with specifics."

---

## Interview Questions Used

1. "How much do you think you spend on AI tools monthly?" (Gauge awareness)
2. "Do you know if you're on the optimal plan?" (Identify pain)
3. "What's your biggest fear with optimizing AI spend?" (Find blocker)
4. "Would you pay for a tool that did this?" (Test pricing)
5. "Who else on your team should use this?" (Test expansion)
6. "What would make you *not* trust our recommendations?" (Find objection)
7. "If we saved you 30% on AI spend, what would you do with the money?" (Test impact)

---

## Recommendations for Product

### Priority 1: Address Trust
- Show *why* each recommendation (compare plans directly)
- Include switching cost warnings
- Show confidence levels (95% vs 60%)

### Priority 2: Add Team Features (Post-MVP)
- Per-person audit view (for CTOs like Marcus)
- Usage tracking if possible
- Benchmarking vs. similar teams

### Priority 3: Make It Safer to Switch
- Offer trial period logic ("Try for 7 days, easy rollback")
- Connection to tool onboarding resources
- Feedback tracking ("Did you switch? How'd it go?")

### Priority 4: Expand Beyond AI
- Sarah mentioned AWS optimization = bigger potential market
- Jessica wanted SaaS tool audit too
- General software spend optimization market is much larger

---

## Red Flags

1. **Nobody said "yes, I'll definitely pay"** for freemium model
   - Implication: Need to prove value before asking for money
   - Recommendation: Free tier + premium only for power users

2. **Switching friction is real**
   - Not just about price, about workflow disruption
   - Recommendation: Minimize disruption in recommendations (consolidate vs. scattered)

3. **Trust is the blocker, not awareness**
   - Marcus thought he was optimized (he wasn't sure)
   - Recommendation: Don't just give recommendations, give proof
