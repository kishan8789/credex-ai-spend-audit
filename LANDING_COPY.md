### FAQ Section

**Q: How accurate are your recommendations?**
> We use official pricing data from each tool and analyze against thousands of team configurations. We're 95%+ accurate. But we always flag uncertain recommendations so you can verify.

**Q: Can you integrate with my billing system?**
> Not yet—we're manual input for MVP. But it's on the roadmap. For now, audits take 3-5 minutes.

**Q: Is my data private?**
> Absolutely. We don't share or sell anything. Your audit data is encrypted and you control who sees it.

**Q: What if I disagree with a recommendation?**
> We show our math. If you think we're wrong, you're probably right—tools have nuances. Use it as a starting point, not gospel.

**Q: Can I share this with my team?**
> Yes! You get a shareable link for your full report (email is hidden for privacy). Or email it to your team.

**Q: What about tools you don't support?**
> Most major AI tools are covered. Custom tools? You can add them and we'll flag them as unsupported.

**Q: Is this free forever?**
> Free tier is always free. We're planning a Premium tier ($29/month) for team re-audits and reports, but basic audits will always be free.

## Copywriting Principles

### Tone
- **Honest**: "You're probably overspending"
- **Helpful**: Specific, actionable advice
- **Founder-focused**: Speak their language (runway, burn rate, efficiency)

### Messaging Hierarchy
1. Problem: Overspending on AI tools
2. Insight: Everyone does it, especially founders
3. Solution: Free 5-minute audit
4. Proof: Real savings examples
5. CTA: Get your audit

### Words to Use
- "Optimize", "Cut", "Save", "Eliminate waste"
- "5 minutes", "Instant", "No setup"
- "Personalized", "Specific", "Based on your team"

### Words to Avoid
- "Disruption", "Revolutionary" (too hype)
- "Complex", "Difficult", "Learning curve"
- "Enterprise", "Advanced" (confuses founders)
